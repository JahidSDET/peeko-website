Getting help faster

When you write about a problem, please include:

  1. Your Peeko version (bottom of the sidebar, or Settings → About).
  2. Your Windows version (press Win + R, type winver, press Enter).
  3. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  4. Your recording settings and a screenshot of any error message.

Many common questions are answered in the FAQ.
