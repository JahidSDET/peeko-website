Peeko Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Peeko is a screen recorder made by Jahidul Islam ("we", "us"). This policy explains what Peeko does with your information. In short: Peeko works entirely on your computer. It does not upload, share or send us anything.

1. What the app does on your computer

What When Where it goes
Screen or window video Only while you are recording Saved as an MP4 file in the folder you choose (default Videos\Peeko)
Microphone audio Only while you are recording with the microphone switched on, and for the live level meter on the Record screen Mixed into your recording; the meter is shown live and never stored
System audio (sound playing on your computer) Only while you are recording with system audio switched on Mixed into your recording
Webcam video Only while the webcam option is on (live preview and recording) Drawn into your recording; the preview is never stored
Mouse click positions Only while you are recording with click highlighting switched on Used for a moment to draw the highlight, then discarded. Peeko never records keystrokes.
Settings (folder, quality, theme…) When you change them %APPDATA%\Peeko\settings.json on your computer
Video thumbnails When you open the Recordings page %APPDATA%\Peeko\thumbnails on your computer

Recording only starts when you start it, and Peeko shows a timer and a recording indicator while it is running.

2. What we do not do

  • Peeko does not connect to the internet. It has no analytics, tracking, advertising, crash reporting or automatic updates.
  • We never receive your recordings, screen contents, audio, webcam images, mouse activity or settings.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

3. Information we do receive

  • When you buy Peeko: your purchase is processed by our merchant of record, Paddle. They collect your name, email address, billing country and payment details to process the order, prevent fraud, handle tax and send your receipt. Their privacy policy applies: https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy. We receive order details (name, email, country, product, price) to provide your licence and support.
  • When you contact support: we receive what you send us (for example your email address, message and any screenshots or files you choose to attach) and use it only to help you.

We keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting law requires (7 years), and support emails for up to 24 months after your issue is resolved.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email jahidul.islam.macomb@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

To delete what Peeko stores on your computer, uninstall Peeko and delete %APPDATA%\Peeko and your recordings folder.

5. Children

Peeko is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at https://peeko.flowgenius.tech/privacy.html.
